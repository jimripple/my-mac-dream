## Design Context

### Users
Church safety operators and a less technical project owner will use this macOS prototype to triage public, reputable safety signals before services and events. The core job is to understand what changed, what is credible, what affects the campus, and what action should happen next.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, mechanical. The app should feel like a serious analyst instrument: confident, restrained, and highly structured. It should not feel like a generic SaaS dashboard, a neon command center, or a playful consumer app.

### Aesthetic Direction
Nothing-inspired industrial minimalism: OLED black, flat monochrome surfaces, hairline borders, dot-matrix structure, Doto hero numerals, Space Grotesk human text, and Space Mono data labels. Dark mode is default; light mode must remain fully usable. Motion should feel percussive and functional.

### Design Principles
- Use motion to explain state changes and confirm interaction, not to decorate.
- Preserve a three-layer hierarchy: posture first, operational widgets second, labels and metadata third.
- Keep red as an earned signal, not an ambient brand color.
- Prefer official/manual intelligence workflows over automated collection until source rules and safety boundaries are defined.
- Make source, entity, and record surfaces clickable into analyst detail views with clear metrics, readout, related records, and next action.
